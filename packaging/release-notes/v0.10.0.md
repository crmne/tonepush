TonePush 0.10.0 brings the Helix Floor: its presets open, its eight setlists back up and show in a picker above the presets, controllers carry the Floor's own names, snapshots show their colours, and models that would not fit on a path are greyed out before the pedal refuses them. Moving or inserting a block now keeps everything that points at it. Crash reports can be turned on, and are off until you do.

## New

- **The Helix Floor works.** Its presets open, including ones with a cleared snapshot name or the Floor's own controller assignments, they are labelled in banks of four (01A to 32D), and a whole-pedal backup reads all eight setlists. Reading, backing up, editing and saving were tested on a Floor at firmware 3.80. By @Jazzpirate in #29, #30, #31 and #32.
- **Choose which setlist the preset list shows.** On a pedal with more than one, a picker sits above the presets. Showing another setlist loads nothing; picking a preset there loads it. By @Jazzpirate in #33.
- **Controllers carry each pedal's own names.** On a Floor, the assignment menu and the Footswitches view offer FS1 to FS11, EXP 1 to 3, the Variax knobs, MIDI CC and Snapshots, and the footswitch board wraps onto more rows instead of squeezing them into one. By @Jazzpirate in #37.
- **Snapshot colours on a Helix Floor.** Each snapshot's colour shows beside its name, and the current snapshot's heading has a menu to change its colour, rename it or copy it over another. By @Jazzpirate in #43.
- **Models that would not fit are greyed out on a Helix Floor.** The model browser shows each model's share of the path's DSP and greys out the ones the pedal would refuse, and the board says how much of each path is in use. By @Jazzpirate in #42.
- **Eight snapshots fit** in the Snapshots view and along the top, where their names used to run over the preset's name. By @Jazzpirate in #35.
- **Crash reports, only if you turn them on.** TonePush asks once, and Settings has a switch. A report says where the editor crashed, on which version and system, and never your name, files, tones or presets. By @crmne.

## Fixed

- **Moving a block keeps its controllers, footswitches and snapshot states.** They used to stay on the slot it left, and on a Helix Floor the saved preset could stop opening. By @Jazzpirate in #40.
- **A block added in a gap lands between the blocks either side of it**, and inserting no longer says a row is full while it has room. It never moves a block across a split or join. By @Jazzpirate and @crmne in #39.
- **An HX Effects keeps sending what happens on the pedal.** It used to fall silent after about 35 seconds until it was power cycled. By @anthonybartczak in #25.
- **A StompStation PRO that is found but cannot be opened says why** on the connect page, instead of "Not found". By @crmne; thanks @Bei894 (#34).
- **Restoring a stored preset on a Helix Floor no longer reports a failure** when the preset landed. By @crmne; thanks @Jazzpirate (#36).
- **A cab's Mic under a controller shows as Mic**, not as the cab's Distance. By @Jazzpirate in #38.

## Known limitations

- Helix LT and Helix Rack have not been tested on real hardware.
- After a TonePush session ends, a Helix Floor's display can freeze until it is power cycled, though it still responds to its switches (#28).
- A Mic assignment can be read, but not changed from TonePush yet.
- An HX Effects backup may still time out while reading the pedal's settings (#9). The Helix Floor fix for the same symptom should cover it, but nobody has tried it on an HX Effects yet.
- An HX Effects refuses `.hlx` imports with error -3 (#44), and an HX Stomp on firmware 3.80 that disconnects after a few seconds (#5) is still being investigated.
- This release still includes the Windows setup programs (`-setup.exe`), so that copies of 0.9.0 can update.
