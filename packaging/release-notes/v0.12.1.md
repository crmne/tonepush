TonePush 0.12.1 finishes what 0.12.0 started for HX Stomps with impulse responses: after a backup reads its IRs, the pedal no longer stays on "transferring data". It also stops a computer with no StompStation PRO from saying one would not open, and lets the AppImage update itself again.

## Fixed

- **An HX Stomp leaves "transferring data" after a backup reads its IRs.** Each IR read is now closed the way an upload is; before, the pedal kept that screen even after TonePush had let it go. Measured on an HX Stomp at 3.80 with 51 IRs; other HX pedals read as before until someone measures them. By @slavashvets in #80.
- **No StompStation PRO plugged in is no longer "would not open".** On a computer without one, the connect page said it was plugged in but would not open; it now keeps looking. By @slavashvets in #82.
- **The AppImage can update itself to and from this release.** 0.12.0's signed list of downloads left the AppImages out, so an AppImage could not verify it. By @crmne.
- **TonePush builds from source on a Mac**, Ruby extension included. By @slavashvets in #81.

## Known limitations

- The HX Stomp fix was measured on macOS only. HX Stomp XL, HX Effects and the Helix pedals have not been measured, so their IR reads are unchanged.
- An HX Stomp on firmware 3.80 that disconnects after a few seconds (#5), and a StompStation PRO the editor cannot open while the command line finds it (#34), are still being investigated. #5 and #79 may both come from reading IRs, which this release and 0.12.0 change.
- Helix LT and Helix Rack have not been tested on real hardware.
